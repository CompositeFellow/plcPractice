## Problem
Design a program to fill and drain a water tank. Pressing normally-open pushbutton (PB1) fills the tank until the water level reaches a normally-open limit switch (FULL). Releasing the pushbutton (PB1) stops the fill operation. Pressing normally-open pushbutton (PB2) drains the tank until the water level reaches normally-open limit switch (LOW). Releasing the pushbutton (PB2) stops the drain operation.
